About Winetime

A direct line between Cambodia and the world's vineyards.

Winetime Cambodia is the official distributor of quality wines and spirits in Cambodia — proudly offering an unparalleled range of products, paired with the best service, every time.

Our promise

Certified, direct-imported, and honestly priced.

Every bottle we sell is sourced directly from the producer — no grey-market detours, no guesswork about provenance. That direct relationship is what lets us guarantee the quality of what's in the glass, and pass on fair pricing instead of stacking layers of middlemen.

We built Winetime because Cambodia's wine lovers deserved the same transparency and service you'd expect from a specialist wine shop anywhere else in the world — with the convenience of ordering from your phone and having it delivered the next day.
