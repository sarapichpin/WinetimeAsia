Cookie Policy

Last updated 2026. This explains what cookies this website uses and why.

What cookies we use

This site keeps things intentionally simple. We use:

  • Essential storage — a small local flag that remembers you've seen this cookie notice, and one that remembers if you've installed or dismissed the app prompt. Stored on your device only, never sent to a server.
  • App/offline storage — if you install this site as an app or revisit it, your browser's service worker caches pages so the site loads faster and works offline.

We do not currently run third-party advertising or tracking cookies on this site. If that changes, this page will be updated and you'll be asked again for consent.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. Declining the cookie notice on this site simply hides the banner — it does not disable essential site functionality.
