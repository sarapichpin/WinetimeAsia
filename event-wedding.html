Good to know

Planning basics

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend reaching out at least 3–4 weeks before your event for weddings, and 1–2 weeks for smaller gatherings — earlier during high season (Nov–Feb).

Can I taste before I decide?

Yes — Diamond and Emerald packages include a tasting session, and we're happy to arrange one for Gold/Platinum on request.

Do you deliver outside Phnom Penh?

Yes, we deliver across Cambodia; lead times outside Phnom Penh may be a little longer. Let us know your venue when you enquire.

What if I don't know quantities yet?

No problem — give us your rough guest count and event type, and we'll suggest a starting order you can adjust closer to the date.
